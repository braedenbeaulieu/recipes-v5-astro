## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup unsalted **butter**
- 4 ounces dark **chocolate**
- 3/4 cup **sugar**
- 2 **eggs**
- 1 teaspoon **vanilla**
- 1/2 cup **flour**
- 1/4 cup **cocoa powder**
- 1/4 teaspoon **salt**

## Tools

- Mini muffin pan
- Mixing bowls
- Rubber spatula
- Small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl

## Directions

### Melt the chocolate

Melt the butter and chocolate together until smooth. Let cool slightly.

### Mix the batter

Whisk in the sugar, eggs, and vanilla. Fold in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt until no dry streaks remain.

### Bake

Portion into a greased mini muffin pan and bake at 350°F for 12 to 14 minutes, until the tops are set.

## Notes

- Let them cool in the pan for 10 minutes before removing.
