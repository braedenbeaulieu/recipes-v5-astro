## Ingredients

- 500 g white **flour**
- 350 g **water**
- 30 g fresh **yeast** or 10 g dry instant yeast
- 10 g sea **salt**

## Tools

- Kitchen scale
- Mixing bowl
- Dough scraper or spoon
- Clean work surface
- Plastic wrap
- Baking sheet
- Second sheet pan (for steam)
- Oven

## Directions

### Mix the dough

Weigh the flour and yeast, then measure the water separately. Combine water with the dry ingredients and mix until the flour is fully hydrated. Let the mixture rest for 5 minutes.

### Add salt and knead

Add sea salt and continue mixing until the dough begins to come together. Once tacky and holding shape, knead on a lightly floured surface for at least 10 minutes until smooth.

### First rise

Form the dough into a large ball and proof for 1–2 hours until it begins to expand.

### Cold fermentation

Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight.

### Shape

The next day, bring the dough close to room temperature. Divide into 250 g portions and shape into baguettes.

### Bake with steam

Score the tops of each baguette and lightly flour them. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Place a second sheet pan in the oven and pour water into it to create steam. Bake the baguettes until golden and crusty.

## Notes

- Longer cold fermentation improves flavour and texture.
- Steam is essential for a crisp crust.
- Let bread cool before slicing for best texture.
