## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose **flour**
- 2 tablespoons **sugar**
- 1 tablespoon **baking powder**
- 1/2 teaspoon **salt**
- 1 1/4 cups **milk**
- 1 **egg**
- 3 tablespoons melted **butter**
- 1 teaspoon **vanilla** extract

## Tools

- Mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Non-stick skillet or griddle
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Spatula

## Directions

### Make the batter

Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined.

### Rest briefly

Let the batter sit for 5 minutes so the flour hydrates and the pancakes cook up a little fluffier.

### Cook

Heat a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat. Add about 1/4 cup batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles form and the edges begin to set, then flip and cook until golden.

## Notes

- Do not overmix; a few lumps are fine.
- Add blueberries or chocolate chips after portioning the batter into the pan.
