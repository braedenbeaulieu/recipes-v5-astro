## Ingredients

- 2 lb **ground beef**
- 2 litres chicken stock or beef **stock**
- 2 tablespoons **olive oil**
- 1 medium **onion**, diced
- 4 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1 can **tomato paste**
- 1 can **diced tomatoes**
- 4 medium **cremini mushrooms**, cut into chunks
- 1 can **black beans**, drained and rinsed
- 1 packet **chili seasoning** or taco seasoning
- **salt** and **pepper**, to taste

## Tools

- Dutch oven or large pot
- Wooden spoon or spatula
- Knife and cutting board
- Measuring spoons

## Directions

### Sauté the aromatics

Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until softened, then add the garlic and cook until fragrant.

### Brown the beef

Add the ground beef and cook until mostly browned. Stir in the chili seasoning and mix well.

### Build the chili

Add the ingredients one at a time, stirring between each addition in this order: tomato paste, cremini mushrooms, black beans, diced tomatoes with their juice, and stock.

### Simmer

Bring the chili to a simmer and cook until it thickens to your desired consistency, stirring occasionally.

## Notes

- For homemade chili seasoning, mix cumin, chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper.
- Simmer longer for deeper flavor and thicker texture.
- Adjust seasoning to taste before serving.
