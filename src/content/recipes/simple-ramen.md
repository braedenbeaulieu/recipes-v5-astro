## Ingredients

- 2 **chicken breasts**
- **salt**, to taste
- **garlic powder**, to taste
- 1 package instant **ramen noodles** (Mr Noodles or similar)
- 2 cups chicken **stock**
- 1/2 cup **bean sprouts**
- 2 sprigs **green onions**, chopped
- 3 teaspoons **soy sauce**
- 1 teaspoon **sesame oil**
- Optional: **chili oil** (for serving)

## Tools

- Frying pan
- Small pot
- Knife and cutting board
- Ladle
- Serving bowls

## Directions

### Cook the chicken

Heat a frying pan over medium heat. Season chicken breasts with salt, garlic powder, and a splash of soy sauce. Fry until fully cooked and lightly browned on the outside. Set aside to rest, then slice.

### Make the broth

Bring chicken stock to a boil in a pot. Add ramen noodles and cook until tender. Stir in the seasoning packet, soy sauce, and minced garlic (if using). Simmer briefly to combine flavors.

### Finish the broth

Stir in sesame oil at the end to add aroma and richness.

### Assemble the bowl

Place noodles and broth into serving bowls. Top with sliced chicken.

### Add toppings

Serve with bean sprouts and chopped green onions on the side or on top. Add chili oil if desired.

## Notes

- Add toppings fresh to keep texture and crunch.
- Adjust soy sauce depending on saltiness of broth.
- For richer flavor, sear chicken until golden before slicing.
