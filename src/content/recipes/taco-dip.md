## Ingredients

- 1 lb **ground beef**
- 1 can **refried beans**
- 1 package Old El Paso **taco seasoning**
- 1 package **Tex-Mex cheese**
- 1 small container **sour cream**
- 2 bags **Tostitos Scoops**
- 2/3 cup **water**

## Tools

- Cast iron pan or oven-safe skillet or just use a normal pan and an oven safe dish

## Directions

### Cook the beef

Brown the ground beef in a cast iron pan over medium heat. Add the taco seasoning and water, then simmer until the mixture thickens slightly. Preheat the oven while it cooks.

### Add the beans

Stir in the refried beans until fully combined and heated through.

### Layer the dip

Turn off the heat. Spread sour cream evenly over the beef mixture, then top with Tex-Mex cheese.

### Bake

Place the pan in the oven and bake until the cheese is fully melted and bubbly.

### Serve

Serve hot with Tostitos Scoops.

## Notes

- If your pan is not oven-safe, transfer the mixture to a baking dish before adding toppings.
- Let the dip cool slightly before serving to help it set.
